My dearest Joy,

Happy 22nd birthday, my love. 🎂

Twenty-two years ago, the world quietly received its greatest gift — and I feel lucky to be the one holding it today. Every year of your life has been worth watching, but these last three? These have been the years my heart learned what it means to be completely, hopelessly, beautifully yours.

We have been through a lot. I remember the fights — the slammed silences, the hurt words, the nights I wondered if we were too much for each other. But Joy, here is what I know for certain: we are never too much for each other. We just have to be patient. We just have to keep choosing forgiveness the way we always do. And we always do. 💕

You have a heart so big it scares me sometimes. You care for patients who will never know your name. You carry worries that aren't yours. You forgive people who don't deserve it — and somehow, you still find time to make me laugh until my stomach hurts.

I want you to know: I see you. I see the nurse, the graduate, the daughter, the friend, the dreamer. I see the girl who got angry at me and then came back with a smile. I see all of it — and I love all of it. The soft parts, the sharp parts, the parts you hide even from yourself.

So today, make a wish. Blow out the candles. And know that whatever this next year brings — the exams, the shifts, the storms, the small ordinary Tuesdays — you will not walk through it alone. I'm right here. I'm not going anywhere. I'm yours, today and in every version of tomorrow I can imagine.

Happy birthday, my love. Here's to 22 — and to every year after, together. 🥂

Forever yours ❤️
